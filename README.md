# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard | Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing coffee shop sales data using Microsoft Excel. An interactive dashboard was developed to monitor sales performance, customer footfall, order trends, product performance, and store-level performance.

The dashboard helps businesses understand customer purchasing behavior, identify sales trends, and make data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall coffee shop sales performance.
- Track total sales and customer footfall.
- Understand average bill and order patterns.
- Identify the best-performing products.
- Analyze sales distribution across product categories.
- Monitor hourly and weekday order trends.
- Compare footfall and sales across store locations.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

**1. Quantity Orders Based on Hours**
- Analyzes order quantity across different hours.
- Identifies peak business hours.
- Helps understand customer ordering patterns.

**2. Category-Wise Sales Distribution**
- Shows percentage contribution of different product categories.
- Helps identify high-performing categories.

**3. Size Distribution Based on Orders**
- Analyzes customer preferences for Large, Regular, and Small sizes.
- Displays order distribution by product size.

**4. Footfall and Sales by Store Location**
- Compares sales and customer footfall across different store locations.
- Helps evaluate individual store performance.

**5. Top 5 Products Based on Sales**
- Identifies the five highest-performing products.
- Helps understand popular products.

**6. Orders on Weekdays**
- Analyzes order volume across weekdays.
- Helps identify customer traffic patterns.
